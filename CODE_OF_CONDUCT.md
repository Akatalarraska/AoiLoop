# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DT1FLOW to be a project where people with Type 1 Diabetes, their
families, carers and anyone else can take part without harassment.

That means everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What is expected

- Be welcoming and patient. A lot of contributors here are patients or parents
  before they are developers.
- Give and take criticism gracefully. Review the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith.
- Respect that people manage their diabetes differently. There is no single
  correct routine, device, or set of habits, and this project does not take
  sides on that.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information, including health information,
  without their explicit permission
- Judging or shaming anyone for their glucose control, device choices,
  weight, diet, or how they manage their condition
- Presenting unsolicited medical advice as fact, or pressuring anyone to change
  their treatment
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate

## About medical advice

Contributors are not each other's clinicians. Do not tell anyone what to do
with their insulin, their pump settings or their glucose targets — in issues,
pull requests, or anywhere else in the project.

Sharing personal experience is fine. Instructing someone is not.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a private security
advisory (which is not public) or contacting a maintainer directly. All reports
are reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate
this code, and may ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful or repeated.
